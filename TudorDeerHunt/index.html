About This Game

This game was inspired by descriptions of King Henry VII and Henry VIII hunting in Simon Thurley's Houses of Power: the Places that Shaped the Tudor World (Penguin Random House 2017), 101-105, 259-262. This iteration of the game was created in November 2026 using human-in-the-loop agentic coding with Fable 5.2 for the game code, GPT 5.4 for sprite generation, and Sonnet 4.6 for planning.

The castle in the background of this game is a 16-bit pixel rendition of Greenwich Castle, a hunting lodge in Greenwich just outside of London. The royals primarily hunted with hawks there, as the park there was too small for hunting deer on horseback. Deer hunting happened instead at places like Woodstock, Hatfield (where they killed 200 deer in one day in 1541), or Waltham (where Henry killed a tame stag and had to pay the lady who owned it 7s 6d).

In this game, deer are being hunted from a standing, where servants drive the deer in front of a static location for people to shoot with crossbows or bows. Normally, Henry would have shot from a grand standing that looked more like a multi-story building than an open wooden platform like you see in the game, but that would have made it impossible for players to see the character and so the game is deliberately historically inaccurate. The food is representative, more than realistic, and the turkey leg is a nod to the ubiquitous Renaissance Faire food rather than how Henry really would have been snacking during such an event. Horns were sounded when a stag approached, to ensure that the people in the standing didn't miss it.

The king's sprite has been generated from his historical portrait. The servants were generated from randomly chosen servants present in The Field of the Cloth of Gold of circa 1545, which is part of the Royal Collection at Hampton Court. The two hunting dogs are from Jacopo Bassano's Two Hounds of circa 1548 now held in the Louvre. The noblewoman was generated from a full-length portrait of Catherine Parr (previously identified as Lady Jane Grey).

Playing with the keyboard

The arrow keys move the crosshair (hold Shift for finer aim). Space or Enter looses a bolt, and also begins the hunt, continues after a fact, and pays her ladyship. The Tab key reaches on-screen buttons for this page, education mode, the music and pausing.

Accessibility options

This game is drawn entirely as moving pictures and cannot be played with a screen reader. Every fact it teaches about Henry VIII can be read as text instead.

Choose Close, or press Escape, to return to the hunt.

The History of Henry VIII

These are the facts the game reveals in education mode, one for each deer taken, in the order a player would meet them. Here they can simply be read. Headings mark each period of Henry's life.
