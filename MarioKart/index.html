Ride through the Tudor roses to win an item. Jump the hay bales. Stay on the road — the houses are slow going. The route follows real streets: the Strand, Fleet Street, Ludgate Hill, St Paul's Churchyard, Watling Street, Budge Row, Candlewick Street, Eastcheap, Tower Street, the Minories, Aldgate, Leadenhall Street, Cornhill, the Poultry, Cheapside, Newgate Street, Snow Hill, Holborn, St Giles and St Martin's Lane. The map shows London c. 1560, a little after Henry's death in 1547. Map image and street lines: the Agas Map edition of The Map of Early Modern London (MoEML), University of Victoria, mapoflondon.uvic.ca.
